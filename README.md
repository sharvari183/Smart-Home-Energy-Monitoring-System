# Smart Home Energy Monitoring System

## Overview

The Smart Home Energy Monitoring System is an industry-oriented IoT and analytics project designed to monitor, analyze, and visualize household energy consumption. The project simulates real-time appliance-level energy usage and provides actionable insights through an interactive dashboard.

This solution demonstrates how smart homes and energy management systems can reduce electricity waste, optimize energy consumption, lower electricity bills, and support sustainable living.

---

## Problem Statement

Households often lack visibility into how electricity is consumed by different appliances. Without monitoring and analytics, energy wastage, high electricity bills, and increased carbon emissions become difficult to control.

This project addresses these challenges by providing:

* Real-time energy monitoring
* Appliance-wise consumption analysis
* Cost estimation
* Carbon footprint tracking
* Smart energy alerts
* Interactive dashboard visualization
